The Nigeria template contains the states and Federal Capital Territory. The Africa and West Africa templates contain country boundaries selected from the world dataset. LearnBridge transforms the source paths only to calculate view boxes and label anchor points; the geographic boundary paths remain from the credited datasets.

The maps are provided for educational illustration. They are not an official source for legal, cadastral, electoral or disputed-boundary decisions.
