# LearnBridge v8.0.4

## Precise diagram labelling

- Replaces bent callout connectors with a single straight leader ending in an arrow at the exact illustrated feature.
- Orders external labels by their real target coordinates rather than AI-suggested box coordinates, preventing same-side line crossings.
- Places label boxes in protected left and right margins with deterministic collision avoidance.
- Draws a white leader halo so pointers remain visible across coloured cells, apparatus, maps, landforms and technical drawings.
- Automatically promotes descriptive free-text labels in cached diagrams to external callouts without another Azure AI request.
- Keeps compact notation such as axis letters, chemical symbols, numbers, units and vertices inside the drawing.
- Limits newly generated illustrations to six callouts per side and requires descriptive names to use callouts.
- Preserves real Nigeria, Africa and West Africa boundary maps.

## Preserved fixes and features

- Native MathML stacked fractions and correctly aligned brackets.
- SMTP password recovery with hashed, single-use, 30-minute reset tokens.
- Nigerian and British curriculum pathways.
- PostgreSQL saved lessons, Paystack subscriptions and school licensing.

The lesson illustration data version remains 3. The renderer advances to version 3, so saved illustrations do not need wholesale regeneration.
